ABOUT GATI SHIKSHA

Welcome to Gati Shiksha

"Gati Shiksha is a revolution in the making — born from the soil of Bharat, shaped by the dreams of its children, and powered by the urgency of change. We are not a startup with a pitch deck. We are a mission with a pulse. In every remote school, every under-equipped classroom, every teacher who wants to do more — Gati Shiksha sees a spark waiting to be ignited."

To empower students and educators across Bharat by making practical STEM education, emerging technologies, and future-ready skills accessible and meaningful for every classroom.

A future where every child, regardless of geography, has the tools and exposure to become an innovator, shaping the technological landscape of tomorrow.
